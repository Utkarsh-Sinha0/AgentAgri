### Interpretation

- **Verifier 100%, safety 90%** — the safety story is intact. Nothing dangerous is reaching farmers.
- **Risk 30%** — `gemma4:e2b-it-q4_K_M` consistently picks lower severity than the golden labels (mostly WATCH where PREVENTIVE_ACTION or ESCALATE is expected). Two paths forward:
  1. **Tune the calibration in `TEMPLATE_SELECTION_PROMPT`** — explicitly anchor risk levels with examples and decision rules.
  2. **Move to a stronger model** (gemma4:e4b, or full e2b precision without Q4 quant) before further prompt work — the small quant may be the bottleneck.
- **Latency** — p50 67 s is too slow for production. CPU + small quant is the cause. GPU + e4b should bring this under 15 s.

## Where to look next

Next session should pick one of:

1. **Prompt-tune `TEMPLATE_SELECTION_PROMPT` for risk calibration** — fastest, free. Run 20-case slice between tweaks (`--limit 20 --categories diagnosis`). Target: risk_match ≥ 50% before bigger model.
2. **Run full 200-case eval on current model as a baseline** — ~50 minutes. Locks in the "before" numbers for any future improvement claim.
3. **Pull gemma4:e4b and re-run the same 10-case smoke** — `ollama pull gemma4:e4b-it-q4_K_M`. Bigger model usually fixes risk calibration without prompt work. Bigger latency hit though.

My recommendation: do **(2) first** (it runs unattended), then **(1)** while it runs.
